Blabber Support

Need help with Blabber? Email us and we’ll do our best to help.

Contact support

Email blabbers.eu@gmail.com and include a short description of the issue. If the problem is with a purchase, contact us from the email address you use for correspondence and include the Apple order ID if available. Never send your Apple Account password or payment card details.

Subscription and purchases

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To view, change, or cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account subscription settings on your device. To restore an eligible purchase, use the restore option in Blabber. If access still does not appear, email support with the approximate purchase date and Apple order ID if available.

App issues

If a feature is not working, try closing and reopening the app and make sure you have an internet connection. When contacting support, tell us your device model, iOS version, app version, what you were doing, and any error message you saw. Please do not include private or sensitive information from your learning scenarios.

Privacy

For information about how Blabber handles data, read our Privacy Policy. For privacy questions or requests, email blabbers.eu@gmail.com.
