Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Blabber handles information when you use the Blabber language-learning application and related services (“Blabber” or the “Service”).

Information the Service handles

Blabber does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app. To provide and protect the Service, it may handle:

  • Language-learning settings and requests: selected languages, learning level, topic, and any custom scenario you submit. A custom scenario may contain information you choose to include.
  • Generated learning content: dialogue text, translations, fictional avatar profiles and images, and generated speech.
  • Installation and security data: an app installation identifier, App Attest key material and assertion counters, session creation and expiry information, and a hashed form of the temporary backend session token.
  • Purchase and subscription data: product identifiers, transaction and original transaction identifiers, subscription status and validity dates, and Apple-signed purchase or notification data needed to verify purchases, restore access, and process subscription events. Payment card details are handled by Apple and are not received by Blabber.
  • Operational information: request identifiers, application and environment identifiers, timestamps, response status, and limited error codes used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service. Prompts, generated responses, purchase payloads, and session credentials are not included in Basis application logs by default.

How information is used

Information is used to generate language-learning materials, synthesize speech, provide and secure anonymous app sessions, enforce usage limits, verify and restore subscriptions, process Apple subscription events, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the Service.

AI and other service providers

When you request a dialogue, the scenario or selected topic and language-learning settings are sent to OpenAI to generate dialogue and fictional avatar content. Avatar descriptions are also used to request generated avatar images from OpenAI. Dialogue text is sent to Google Gemini to synthesize speech. The generated content is returned to the app.

Blabber explicitly enables storage for its OpenAI Responses API text-generation requests and outputs so they can be inspected in OpenAI Platform Logs. OpenAI handles that content under its applicable API terms and data-retention practices. Image-generation requests use a separate OpenAI API and are reflected in provider usage records. Google handles speech-generation inputs under the applicable Gemini API terms. Do not submit sensitive personal information in a scenario or other prompt.

Apple processes App Store purchases and provides signed transaction and subscription event information that Blabber uses to verify access. Service hosting and database providers process information as needed to run the API and store subscription, entitlement, security, and usage records. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable agreements.

These providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards for international transfers will be used.

Storage and retention

Generated dialogue, avatar images, and speech are cached in server memory for approximately one hour and are not stored as durable Blabber content on the server. The app may download and keep learning materials on your device; those local copies remain until you remove them or delete the app.

Installation security records, subscription verification records, usage records, and Apple notification records are stored in the service database for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain security and service integrity, resolve subscription issues, and meet legal obligations. Backend sessions expire after 30 days. Expired session records and other records may remain for operational, security, or legal purposes.

OpenAI may retain text-generation requests and outputs in its Platform Logs according to its applicable policies and settings. Provider retention is governed by the relevant provider’s terms and controls, rather than by Blabber’s approximately one-hour server-memory cache.

Sharing and advertising

Information is shared with the service providers described above only as needed to operate the Service, verify purchases, provide AI features, maintain security, or comply with law. Blabber does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Blabber does not use the information it handles for cross-app tracking.

Your choices and requests

You can avoid including personal or sensitive information in custom scenarios. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account settings. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of Blabber by emailing the privacy contact below. Information may be needed to locate the relevant records and verify the request. Some transaction, security, or operational records may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to protect the Service and resolve disputes.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law, including rights to object to or restrict certain processing and to lodge a complaint with a privacy regulator. Email the privacy contact below to exercise applicable rights.

Children

Blabber is not intended for children under 13, and personal information is not knowingly collected directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Service, email the privacy contact below so the request can be reviewed.

Security

Blabber uses App Attest to help verify supported app installations and signed requests, stores backend session tokens as hashes, and limits access to subscription and usage records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Service or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel where required.
